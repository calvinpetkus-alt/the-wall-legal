Privacy Policy

The Wall · Last updated July 12, 2026

The Wall (“the app”) is a lacrosse wall-ball rep counter. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the control you have over your data. We collect as little as possible.

The microphone

The app uses your phone’s microphone to detect the sound of the ball hitting the wall so it can count your reps hands-free. Audio is analyzed only on your device, in real time. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. No sound leaves your phone.

What we store

  • Your sessions — rep counts, duration, dates, and workout details.
  • Your profile — the username, position, experience level, and optional profile photo you enter.
  • Your settings — goals and preferences.

All of this lives on your phone first. If you sign in, a copy is backed up to our cloud provider so you don’t lose it if you lose your phone.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. We receive an anonymous Apple ID used only to link your account to your backup. We do not receive or store your password, and we do not use your email for marketing.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Cloud storage and authentication are handled by Supabase and Apple as our service providers. If you add friends, other users can see your username and public stats.

No third-party tracking

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → About → Delete account inside the app. This erases your data from both your phone and the cloud and cannot be undone.

Children

The app is intended for athletes age 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
